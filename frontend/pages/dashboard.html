About Modern Furniture Pacific

Crafting Excellence Since 2010

Modern Furniture Pacific is a premier furniture design and manufacturing company based in Kenya. With over 15 years of experience, we specialize in creating luxurious, handcrafted furniture that combines traditional craftsmanship with modern innovation.

Our mission is to transform living spaces with premium quality furniture that stands the test of time. Every piece is designed with attention to detail and made from the finest materials available.
